## Problems with Cloud and DIY Automation

### Execution Caps and Surprise Bills

Most cloud platforms cap you at 2,500 executions per month. A single "check for new emails every 5 minutes" workflow burns through 8,000 executions. Go over and you pay more, or your workflows stop dead. Awesomate uses infrastructure-based pricing — run 10,000 or 100,000 workflows with no surprise bills and no throttling.

### Shared Servers and Shared Problems

Cloud platforms typically provide 320MB of RAM with burstable CPU shared with everyone else. One heavy workflow processing a medium-sized CSV and your entire instance crashes, often silently with no clear error message. Awesomate deploys on Kubernetes with dedicated resources and 99.99% uptime.

### Disappearing Data

Cloud starter plans retain execution logs for only 7 days. Weekly backups overwrite themselves. When you need to debug why a lead didn't sync two weeks ago, that data is already gone forever. Awesomate runs backups every 30 minutes with triple-live-copy redundancy.

### Forum Support Isn't Real Support

When your automation breaks on a Friday afternoon, "post to the community forum" isn't an answer. Most platforms don't troubleshoot your specific setup — they tell you the platform is working. Awesomate provides concierge onboarding, ticketed support, workflow design help, and 24/7 monitoring.

## n8n Hosting FAQ

### What does "uncapped executions" actually mean?

Most cloud platforms charge per execution or cap you at a set number per month. Awesomate uses infrastructure-based pricing — you pay for the server resources, not per workflow run. Whether you run 10,000 or 100,000 executions, your bill stays the same.

### How is the infrastructure different from shared cloud hosting?

Cloud platforms typically run your instance on shared servers with as little as 320MB of RAM. Awesomate deploys on Kubernetes with dedicated resources, and your database runs as three HOT replicas — live copies that can accept traffic at any time. If one node goes down, failover happens in 1–2 seconds with no manual intervention. Local snapshots run every 30 minutes and offsite backups provide point-in-time recovery.

### Where is my data hosted?

Your data is hosted on OVHcloud infrastructure — the world's #5 hosting provider — in enterprise-grade US data centres. Most cloud platforms default to Frankfurt, Germany with no option to choose. Awesomate offers regional flexibility: if your business needs hosting in Asia Pacific, Europe, or a specific jurisdiction, deployment can be arranged there.

Yes. You get the full power of self-hosting — custom nodes, NPM packages, bash scripts, environment variables, and full configuration control. Cloud platforms restrict these to self-hosted plans only. Awesomate gives you all of it without the DevOps burden.

### What happens if a server goes down?

Your database runs as three HOT replicas at all times — live copies ready to accept traffic instantly. If any node experiences an issue, failover happens in 1–2 seconds. Local snapshots run every 30 minutes (held for a rolling 3 days) and offsite backups provide point-in-time recovery to any minute in the past (rolling 10 days).
